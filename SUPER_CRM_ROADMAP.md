# Bizavo super CRM roadmap

Zoho succeeds through breadth, but its breadth is split across products. Bizavo's strategy is a shared data backbone with industry packs, role-based navigation and complete transaction chains.

## Capability map

| Domain | Working now | Next complete slice | Later depth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CRM | Website leads and client data on projects | Contacts, companies, opportunity pipeline, tasks and activity timeline | Campaigns, scoring, cadences, customer portal |
| Projects | Contracts, milestones, phases, documents and project P&L | BOQ, change orders and client progress portal | Scheduling dependencies and portfolio capacity |
| Purchases | Vendors, approval-based POs, receipts and payables | Purchase returns, vendor credits and approval policies | Vendor portal, tenders and price agreements |
| Inventory | Multi-location stock, transfers, adjustments, issues and weighted average | Cycle counts, reservations and barcode workflows | Serial/batch, composite items and demand planning |
| People | Directory, assignments, attendance and leave | Employee onboarding, documents and attendance regularization | Performance, OKRs, learning and engagement |
| Payroll | Salary structure, monthly processing, payslips and payment posting | Variable earnings, reimbursements, loans and payroll review/approval | India statutory engine, filings and bank payouts |
| Finance | Double-entry books, AR/AP, expenses, statements and project P&L | Credit notes, reversals, bank reconciliation and period locks | GST/TDS workflows, bank feeds and multi-currency |
| Expenses | Direct project/organization expenses | Employee claims, receipt upload, policy and approval | OCR, card feeds, travel and automated reimbursement |
| Documents | Receipts, quotes, proforma invoices, PDF and secure delivery | Templates, branding controls and recurring documents | E-sign, versioning and customer/vendor portals |
| Automation | Milestone invoice, receipt, journal and entitlement automation | Rule builder for status, reminders and approvals | Webhooks, custom functions and integration marketplace |

## Build rule

Each slice must include schema, tenant isolation, permissions, empty/error states, seed data, audit history, tests and production verification. A connected workflow that works end to end is preferred over several placeholder menu items.
